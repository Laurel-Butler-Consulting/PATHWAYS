If you are a credentialed non-arts teacher and you want to teach the arts, one pathway is to add a Supplementary Authorization to your existing credential. For multiple subject credential holders, adding a Supplementary Authorization allows you to teach the arts in grade 9 and below. You'll need 20 semester units or 10 upper division semester units in the content area.

For example, a dance authorization must include classes from the dance department, and only units from institutions of higher education are allowed. The advantage is you don't need any classes or exams, and the application is made directly to the CTC. For Single Subject Credential holders, you can follow the same process to add a specific Supplementary Authorization. Or, if you choose, you can add a second Single Subject Credential by demonstrating subject matter competency and taking a one-semester methods course in an approved teacher education program.

You can prove subject matter competency through a degree in dance, music, theatre, or visual arts, by taking the CSET, or through a combination of college coursework and one CSET. So how do you begin? Review your transcripts and consult the CTC website for specific information regarding Supplementary Authorizations. Because individual circumstances can vary, and there are some limitations on what subjects can be taught, we recommend reviewing the CTC guidance and contacting the Commission directly with specific questions. In general though, the process is more straightforward than you might think.
