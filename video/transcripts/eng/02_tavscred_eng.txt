What's the difference between a teaching artist and a credentialed arts teacher? Both roles are essential to arts education, but they're different in some important ways, and knowing the distinction will help you figure out which path is right for you. 

A teaching artist is a practicing artist who brings their craft into educational settings like classrooms, after school programs, community organizations. It's flexible, it's usually project based, it's usually freelance. 

A credentialed arts teacher, on the other hand, holds a state issued credential to teach in a public school - they're a classroom teacher. This can be a full time job with benefits and union protections. 

Neither path is better than the other, it just depends on how you want to structure your career, where you want your art to live, how much flexibility or stability you want. You might start as a teaching artist and pursue a credential later, so pick the option that feels right for you and we'll help you figure out what fits.
