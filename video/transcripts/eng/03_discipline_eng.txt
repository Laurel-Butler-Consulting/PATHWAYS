One of the first and most exciting questions you'll answer is: "What do you want to teach?" 

In California, arts credentials are divided into 4 distinct subject areas: Dance, Theater, Music, and Visual Arts. Each is its own credential. That means you'll be credentialed in your specific discipline, not just the arts generally, which also means the pathway may look a little different depending on which one you choose.

Some of these subject areas have more credentialing programs available statewide than others. They might have different exam requirements or different demand in the schools, so it's worth knowing what you're walking into.

If you're not sure which one fits you best — like if you work across multiple art forms — just pick one to start with. You can always go back into the portal to try alternate pathways and decide which one feels best for you.
