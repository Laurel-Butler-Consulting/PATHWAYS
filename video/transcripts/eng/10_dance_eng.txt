To get a single subject dance credential in California, most people start by earning a college degree in dance. This shows the state that you have subject matter competency, which I know you already do. This is especially important for the dance credential because the programs in the state teach you how to teach, but not what to teach - that part comes from you.

If you don't have a degree in dance, but you took at least 32 units of coursework in college that is dance related, that will work too. Once you have your bachelor's degree, you will apply to and enroll in a program to get your single subject credential in dance. And there are now more than 13 programs all over the state, from Sacramento to San Diego, where you can do just that.

Some are totally remote or online, some are fully in person, and some have hybrid options. Take a look at the programs and decide which one makes the most sense for you. Once you're in a single subject credential program, we'd also recommend getting a PE credential as well. That way, you'll be able to offer a dance class, and your students can receive either dance or PE credits for the course. This makes you super marketable for schools, and learning more is always a good thing.
