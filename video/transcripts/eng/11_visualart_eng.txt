It's an exciting time to become a visual art teacher in California. Single subject art credential program applications vary at different institutions. For example, some schools require a portfolio review of your own artwork, while others don't. So be sure to check the admissions criteria for each program to know what you'll need to apply. 

At minimum, most programs will ask you for subject matter competency in art, early field experiences, and an interview. Subject matter competency is showing what you know about art. You can earn a degree in art, take the CSET in art, complete an approved subject matter program in art, or do a combination of the exam and coursework. Early field experiences include observations in K-12 schools, or other settings where you've observed or worked with kids. 

Once you're in, there's usually a few different program pathways that you can choose from. For example, at CSUN, we have an accelerated one-year program as well as the traditional program, which is about 3 to 4 semesters with student teaching. We also have an internship program for people who can find employment in a public school setting while working towards their credential.

Read through the list of programs available. There sure to be one out there that's perfect for you.
