Ok, I'm here to walk you through what it takes to earn a single subject music credential in California. There are three ways to get there, depending on where you are starting from. The first is the most direct and the most complete. You earn a Single Subject Credential in music, which authorizes you to teach music at any grade level Pre-K through 12.

To do that, you need a bachelor's degree, subject matter competency in music, and completion of a credential program that includes a music methods course and student teaching. Subject matter competency simply means demonstrating that you know music. Most candidates do that either through a music degree or by passing the CSET in music. 

The other options are for teachers who already hold a credential: you can add a Supplementary Authorization in music through coursework alone - no methods course, no student teaching required. Or you can add music as a full second credential, which requires subject matter competency and a methods course. This is the option districts recognize the most when hiring for secondary music. If you're just starting out, the goal is to get that full credential - and the earlier you plan your coursework, the smoother that path will be.
