If you've been working professionally in your art form, you may already be closer to teaching than you think. California has a special pathway called a CTE credential (Career Technical Education) and it's designed specifically for working professionals who want to bring their real world expertise into the classroom. 

Here's the key requirement:  if you have at least 1000 hours of paid experience per year for at least 3 years, you may qualify.

CTE pathways are typically faster, more accessible, and affordable than a traditional teaching credential. They're built around the idea that your professional experience is your qualification. This can be a great fit for artists, designers, performers, or industry professionals who want to teach without going back to school for several years. Does this sound like you?
