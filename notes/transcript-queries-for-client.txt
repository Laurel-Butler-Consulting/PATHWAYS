ARTS EDUCATOR PATHWAYS — TRANSCRIPT QUERIES FOR CLIENT
Prepared 2026-08-04. 

These are wording questions, not corrections. Nothing in this list has been changed on the
site — each one needs a decision from you or your translator. Clear mechanical errors were
corrected separately; those are listed at the end for your records.


SPANISH — MEANING

1.  VIDEO 5, CTE INTRO
    As written: "...profesionales en activo que quieren llevar su experiencia del mundo
    del aula."
    The English says these professionals bring their real-world expertise INTO the
    classroom. The Spanish as written reads closer to "experience from the world of the
    classroom", which reverses the point of the sentence.
    Suggested: "...llevar su experiencia del mundo real al aula."
    This is the one item on the list that changes what a viewer understands.

2.  VIDEOS 5 AND 6, CTE INTRO AND CTE PATH
    As written: "Carrera Técnica Educacional" as the translation of Career Technical
    Education.
    The words appear in a different order from the English term, and this is not the
    rendering normally used in California materials. The earlier draft used "Educación
    Técnica Profesional".
    Please confirm which term you want, and note that it must match on both videos.


SPANISH — REGISTER AND CONSISTENCY
(All of these are grammatical as written. They are style calls that belong to your
translator, not errors.)


3.  VIDEO 8, MUSIC
    As written: "...la meta es conseguir la credencial completa - lo más antes
    planifiques tus cursos, más fácil será el camino."
    "Lo más antes" is not the standard construction; "cuanto antes planifiques" is the
    usual form. A similar phrase does circulate colloquially in parts of Latin America,
    so this may be a deliberate spoken register.

4.  VIDEO 3, DISCIPLINE
    As written: "Una de las primeras y más emocionantes preguntas a que vas a
    responder..."
    Correct but formal, and slightly stiff against the conversational tone elsewhere.
    Alternative: "...preguntas que vas a responder..."

5.  VIDEO 3, DISCIPLINE
    As written: "...no en las artes en general, que también significa que el camino
    puede verse un poco diferente..."
    Careful writing would use "lo que también significa". Dropping it is very common in
    speech, so this may be intentional for a spoken script.

6.  VIDEO 7, SUPPLEMENTARY AUTHORIZATION
    As written: "...el proceso es más sencillo de lo que tal vez pensarás."
    The future tense is unusual in this comparison. More idiomatic: "de lo que piensas"
    or "de lo que pensarías".

7.  VIDEO 5, CTE INTRO
    As written: "...ya estés más cerca de ser arte educador de lo que crees."
    Masculine form. The rest of the Spanish set consistently addresses the viewer with
    feminine forms (maestra, educadora, artista docente). Please confirm whether the
    masculine here is deliberate or should follow the rest of the set.

8.  VIDEO 10, DANCE
    As written: "Algunos son totalmente remotos en el internet..."
    "En línea" is the more usual phrase for online study.

9.  VIDEO 11, VISUAL ART
    As written: "experiencias tempranas en el campo" in one sentence, then "experiencias
    tempranas de campo" two sentences later, for the same thing.
    Please pick one and use it in both places.

V3 — NEW TRANSLATION, RECEIVED 2026-08-06
Logged 2026-08-06.

Your translator's new Spanish replaced the V2 text entirely. The eleven transcript files
and the site were rebuilt from it. This section records what was changed in it, and what
was deliberately left alone.


ONE MEANING CHANGE — PLEASE READ

  Video 5, CTE Intro. This is item 1 from the V2 list above. It is unchanged in the new
  translation, so rather than query it a second time it has been corrected.

    As delivered:  "...que quieren llevar su experiencia del mundo del sala de clase."
    Now reads:     "...que quieren llevar su experiencia del mundo real a la sala de clase."

  The English is "bring their real world expertise into the classroom." As delivered, the
  Spanish said the experience came FROM the world of the classroom, reversing the point of
  the sentence — and it is the sentence that explains what CTE is for. It also becomes a
  subtitle, so it could not be left as written.


A DROPPED REQUIREMENT — PLEASE READ

  Video 5, CTE Intro. The new translation removed the time requirement from the key
  sentence and replaced it with something else. The V2 wording has been restored.

    English (and the on-screen graphic):
      "...at least 1000 hours of work experience PER YEAR FOR AT LEAST 3 YEARS..."
    V3 as delivered:
      "...al menos 1000 horas de experiencia laboral EN TU DISCIPLINA ARTÍSTICA, O EN UNA
       INDUSTRIA RELACIONADA..."
    Now reads (V2 wording, restored):
      "...al menos 1000 horas de experiencia laboral POR AÑO DURANTE AL MENOS 3 AÑOS..."

  As delivered, the Spanish said 1000 hours; the requirement is 1000 hours a year for three
  years. That is a materially different — and much lower — bar.

  It matters more in this video than anywhere else on the site. The requirement is shown as
  an on-screen graphic in English, which is why the Spanish subtitles carry three extra cues
  at that point. Those cues are the only way a Spanish viewer receives the requirement at
  all, so an incomplete version there is not recoverable from anything else on screen.


WORDING CHANGED ON YOUR INSTRUCTION

  Video 5, CTE Intro.   "experiencia remunerada"  ->  "experiencia laboral"

  The Commission counts three years of work experience "full-time or part-time, paid or
  unpaid", so the word "paid" turns away people who actually qualify. The narration, the
  on-screen graphic and the English transcript were all settled on "work experience" on
  2026-08-06, and the Spanish now matches. Video 6, CTE Path, already said "experiencia
  laboral" and is unchanged.


NUMBERS

  The 1000-hour figure appeared on the site three different ways — "1000", "1,000" and
  "1,000 horas". It is now written 1000 everywhere, with no comma. Three instances in the
  Spanish were affected (Videos 1, 5 and 6). No wording changed.
