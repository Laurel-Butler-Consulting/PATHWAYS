These are wording questions, not corrections. Nothing in this list has been changed on the

SPANISH — MEANING

1.  VIDEO 5, CTE INTRO
    As written: "...profesionales en activo que quieren llevar su experiencia del mundo
    del aula."
    The English says these professionals bring their real-world expertise INTO the
    classroom. The Spanish as written reads closer to "experience from the world of the
    classroom", which reverses the point of the sentence.
    Suggested: "...llevar su experiencia del mundo real al aula."
    This is the one item on the list that changes what a viewer understands.

2.  VIDEOS 5 AND 6, CTE INTRO AND CTE PATH
    As written: "Carrera Técnica Educacional" as the translation of Career Technical
    Education.
    The words appear in a different order from the English term, and this is not the
    rendering normally used in California materials. The earlier draft used "Educación
    Técnica Profesional".
    Please confirm which term you want, and note that it must match on both videos.


SPANISH — REGISTER AND CONSISTENCY
(All of these are grammatical as written. They are style calls that belong to your
translator, not errors.)


3.  VIDEO 8, MUSIC
    As written: "...la meta es conseguir la credencial completa - lo más antes
    planifiques tus cursos, más fácil será el camino."
    "Lo más antes" is not the standard construction; "cuanto antes planifiques" is the
    usual form. A similar phrase does circulate colloquially in parts of Latin America,
    so this may be a deliberate spoken register.

4.  VIDEO 3, DISCIPLINE
    As written: "Una de las primeras y más emocionantes preguntas a que vas a
    responder..."
    Correct but formal, and slightly stiff against the conversational tone elsewhere.
    Alternative: "...preguntas que vas a responder..."

5.  VIDEO 3, DISCIPLINE
    As written: "...no en las artes en general, que también significa que el camino
    puede verse un poco diferente..."
    Careful writing would use "lo que también significa". Dropping it is very common in
    speech, so this may be intentional for a spoken script.

6.  VIDEO 7, SUPPLEMENTARY AUTHORIZATION
    As written: "...el proceso es más sencillo de lo que tal vez pensarás."
    The future tense is unusual in this comparison. More idiomatic: "de lo que piensas"
    or "de lo que pensarías".

7.  VIDEO 5, CTE INTRO
    As written: "...ya estés más cerca de ser arte educador de lo que crees."
    Masculine form. The rest of the Spanish set consistently addresses the viewer with
    feminine forms (maestra, educadora, artista docente). Please confirm whether the
    masculine here is deliberate or should follow the rest of the set.

8.  VIDEO 10, DANCE
    As written: "Algunos son totalmente remotos en el internet..."
    "En línea" is the more usual phrase for online study.

9.  VIDEO 11, VISUAL ART
    As written: "experiencias tempranas en el campo" in one sentence, then "experiencias
    tempranas de campo" two sentences later, for the same thing.
    Please pick one and use it in both places.
