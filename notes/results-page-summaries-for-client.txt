SUPPLEMENTARY AUTHORIZATION
---------------------------

DESCRIPTION:
    Adds an arts subject to a California teaching credential you already hold.
    Multiple Subject holders get an Introductory authorization, limited to
    material taught in grade 9 and below. Single Subject holders can get a
    Specific authorization, covering the subject at any level, preschool
    through 12 and adult classes.

REQUIREMENTS:
    A California teaching credential: Multiple Subject, Standard Elementary,
    Single Subject, Standard Secondary or Special Secondary.

QUALIFICATIONS:
    20 semester units, or 10 upper division units, of non-remedial coursework
    in the subject from a regionally accredited community college or
    university. Alternatives: a collegiate major in a directly related
    subject, the relevant CSET, or a coursework and examination
    combination through a program approved by the Commission on Teacher
    Credentialing.

PROCESS:
    No preparation program or examination required. Apply directly to the
    Commission with form 41-4, official transcripts and the fee.

NOTES:
    A letter from the subject department chair may be requested if a course's
    relevance is unclear. Single Subject holders may instead add a second
    Single Subject credential by verifying subject matter competency and
    completing a methods course of at least three semester units.

==============================================================================

TEACHING ARTIST
---------------

DESCRIPTION:
    A practicing artist who teaches in schools, museums, community
    organizations and after-school settings. No state credential required.

REQUIREMENTS:
    None set by the state. Set independently by employers and host
    organizations.

QUALIFICATIONS:
    No credential or examination. Organizations commonly look for training in
    classroom management, lesson planning, arts integration and
    social-emotional learning.

PROCESS:
    Training programs, some run by non-profits that also hire teaching artists
    and place them in schools. No state application, examination or fee.

NOTES:
    Work is typically freelance and project-based, from single workshops to
    longer classroom residencies. Learning to market your services is
    important.

CAREER TECHNICAL EDUCATION (CTE) CREDENTIAL
-------------------------------------------

DESCRIPTION:
    Teaching authorization based on your professional experience. Covers
    career technical, trade and vocational courses in the named subject,
    in grades 12 and below and adult classes.

REQUIREMENTS:
    A high school diploma, GED or foreign equivalent, plus fingerprint
    clearance and CPR training.

QUALIFICATIONS:
    Three years of employer-verified work experience in the industry sector,
    at least 1,000 clock hours each year, full or part time, paid or
    unpaid. One year must fall within the last five, or two within the
    last ten. Up to two years may be substituted by 48 semester units of
    related postsecondary vocational training.

PROCESS:
    Apply through an approved program sponsor, which recommends you for the
    preliminary credential. It is valid three years. Clearing it takes two
    years of successful teaching plus a program approved by the Commission
    on Teacher Credentialing covering the US Constitution, health
    education and computer technology.

NOTES:
    No university degree is required, and the credential is completed while
    teaching. Holders commonly continue their arts practice alongside the
    classroom work.

SINGLE SUBJECT CREDENTIAL IN MUSIC
----------------------------------

DESCRIPTION:
    Authorizes teaching music in departmentalized classes at any grade level,
    preschool through 12, and adult classes.

REQUIREMENTS:
    A bachelor's degree from a regionally accredited college or university, in
    a field other than professional education.

QUALIFICATIONS:
    Subject matter competency in music: a music degree major, a subject matter
    program approved by the Commission on Teacher Credentialing, the CSET
    in Music, coursework verified by an approved preparation program, or a
    coursework and examination combination for candidates already enrolled
    in a program.

PROCESS:
    Complete a Commission-approved preparation program, including a music
    methods course, student teaching and a Teaching Performance
    Assessment. Also complete a US Constitution course or examination and
    receive a formal recommendation from the program.

NOTES:
    Planning subject coursework early shortens the path, since any gaps must
    be closed before competency can be verified.

REQUIREMENTS:
    A bachelor's degree from a regionally accredited college or university, in
    a field other than professional education.

QUALIFICATIONS:
    Subject matter competency in theatre: a theatre degree major, a subject
    matter program approved by the Commission on Teacher Credentialing,
    the CSET in Theatre, coursework verified by an approved preparation
    program, or a coursework and examination combination for candidates
    already enrolled in a program.

PROCESS:
    Complete a Commission-approved preparation program, including a theatre
    methods course, student teaching and a Teaching Performance
    Assessment. Also complete a US Constitution course or examination and
    receive a formal recommendation from the program.

NOTES:
    The coursework route, which programs call transcript evaluation, has a
    program check your transcripts against the required subject domains.
    Professional experience and training taken outside higher education do
    not count. Prior teaching experience is not required for admission.

SINGLE SUBJECT CREDENTIAL IN DANCE
----------------------------------

DESCRIPTION:
    Authorizes teaching dance in departmentalized classes at any grade level,
    preschool through 12, and adult classes.

REQUIREMENTS:
    A bachelor's degree from a regionally accredited college or university, in
    a field other than professional education.

QUALIFICATIONS:
    Subject matter competency in dance: a dance degree major, a subject matter
    program approved by the Commission on Teacher Credentialing, the CSET
    in Dance, coursework verified by an approved preparation program, or a
    coursework and examination combination for candidates already enrolled
    in a program.

PROCESS:
    Complete a Commission-approved preparation program, including student
    teaching and a Teaching Performance Assessment. Also complete a US
    Constitution course or examination and receive a formal
    recommendation. Programs run statewide in online, in-person and hybrid
    formats.

NOTES:
    The CSET in Dance has only existed since October 2021, so older guidance
    may state that no examination is available. Adding a physical
    education credential lets a dance class carry either dance or PE
    credit, widening hiring options.
