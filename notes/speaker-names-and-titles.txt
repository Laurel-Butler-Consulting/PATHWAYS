Worst case is ~63px of text before padding, about 10% of the picture on a phone. Every speaker

name and one role. All strips share one box size, so the four-line speakers set it for all eight.

THREE DECISIONS STILL OPEN — nothing is built until these are answered

1. ONE TITLE LINE OR BOTH.
   Name plus the first line gives every speaker exactly two lines and roughly halves the box. The
   second line is usually the institutional affiliation, which the 2026-08-06 note argued belongs on
   a result page rather than across the presenter. Recommended: name + first title only.

2. THE TWO-PRESENTER NODES (welcome, discipline).
   Both names at once, or each name as that person speaks? Per-speaker timing means cue points for
   every clip — real work, and it cannot be built until the clips are in. Both-at-once is one static
   strip but doubles its height on the node people see first.
   Recommended: both names together, no per-speaker switching.

3. HOW LONG IT HOLDS.
   The approved mock-up fixed the POSITION — above the caption line at a fixed offset, so nothing
   moves when captions appear — but never settled duration. Recommended: fade after about 5 seconds,
   so it stops competing with the captions for the rest of the clip.

BUILD CONSTRAINTS, carried from the 2026-08-06 mock-up decision

- DRAW THE STRIP IN THE SITE, do not burn it into the video. On a small phone the bottom 423px of a
  1080×1920 export is cropped away before the player's own furniture takes more, so a burned-in
  strip would have to sit ~60% up the frame to survive — which reads as a floating label, not a
  lower third. Drawing it also means a misspelled name is a text edit, not a re-cut, and the job
  title can be translated.
- CAPTIONS DO NOT MOVE. Move the transient element, never the persistent one.
- The copy belongs in data/content.en.json with the rest of the text, not in index.html.
