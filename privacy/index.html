Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Who we are. Arts Educator Pathways is an initiative of CREATE CA in partnership with Laurel Butler Consulting. The site and any information you give us are the responsibility of Laurel Butler Consulting. Questions about this policy: info@createca.org.

What we collect. Your email address, and only if you choose to give it to us on the sign-up form. Nothing else.

We never sell, rent, or share your email address, and we don't use it for advertising. Every email you receive from us will have an unsubscribe link. You can unsubscribe at any time. Ask us and we'll delete your address from our records.

What we don't collect. We don't use advertising, tracking pixels, or cookies that follow you around. We don't build a profile of you. Your answers to the questionnaire stay on your device — they are never sent to us or stored anywhere. Settings like caption language and playback speed are saved in your own browser and never leave it.

Visitor statistics. We use Plausible, a privacy-focused analytics service, to see how many people use the site and which parts are useful. It sets no cookies, never stores your IP address, and cannot identify you or follow you between sites or from one day to the next. The figures we see are counts, not people.

Who else sees your information. Buttondown stores our email list and sends the emails; they may not use your address for their own purposes. Plausible processes visitor statistics in the European Union. GitHub hosts the site and keeps standard server logs including IP addresses, as every web host does.
